## Project Overview

This project presents comprehensive keyword research conducted for FreeInternships.in as part of the Digital Marketing Internship at Data Alcott Systems.

The objective of this project is to identify relevant keywords, analyze their search volume and keyword difficulty, understand search intent, study competitors, identify keyword opportunities, organize keywords into topic clusters, and develop a content strategy for the website.

---

## Objectives

- Identify relevant seed keywords for FreeInternships.in
- Expand seed keywords into related and long-tail keywords
- Identify question-based keywords
- Analyze search volume and keyword difficulty
- Analyze search intent and competition
- Study competitor keywords
- Identify keyword opportunities and gaps
- Create keyword clusters and content pillars
- Prioritize keywords
- Develop a keyword-based content calendar
- Provide SEO recommendations
